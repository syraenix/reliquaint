# Code of Conduct

Reliquaint is a project about caring for old things — software, the people who made it, and the people who still play it. The same care extends to our community. Whether you're contributing code, a manifest, a walkthrough, or just asking a question in an issue, this is what's expected of you and what you can expect from others.

## What we ask of everyone

- **Treat people as collaborators, not opponents.** Disagree with ideas; don't attack the person.
- **Assume good faith.** Most miscommunication is just miscommunication.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Everyone was new to retro gaming, or to Rust, or to TOML, or to git, at some point. A patient answer is worth more than a clever one.
- **Be patient with response times.** This is a volunteer project. Maintainers and contributors have day jobs, families, gardens, and other obligations.
- **Respect the legal lines.** Don't ask for, link to, or share game files you don't have the right to distribute, and don't pressure others to.

## What is not OK

The following will not be tolerated in any Reliquaint space — issues, pull requests, discussions, related chat venues, or events:

- Harassment, intimidation, or targeted personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior, including but not limited to remarks based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or nationality.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, particularly in any context involving minors.
- Trolling, baiting, or bad-faith argumentation dressed up as "just asking questions."

## Enforcement

Reports of behavior that violates this code can be sent to the project maintainer at `syraenix@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed and responded to in good faith, with the reporter's identity kept confidential where possible.

Consequences for violations escalate with severity and pattern:

- **First instance, minor:** a private clarification of expectations.
- **Continued or moderate:** a temporary ban from project spaces.
- **Severe or repeated:** a permanent ban from project spaces.

The maintainer's decision is final for the project's own spaces. People banned from Reliquaint spaces may, of course, continue to use the software — this code governs participation in the community, not access to the tool.

## Scope

This code applies in all official Reliquaint spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), any project chat channels, and any in-person or online events held under the Reliquaint name. It also applies when a contributor's behavior outside these spaces could reasonably affect the safety of the Reliquaint community.
